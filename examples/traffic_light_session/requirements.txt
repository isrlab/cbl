A traffic light controller manages an intersection.

The system reads two boolean inputs: timer_expired (from a hardware timer)
and pedestrian_request (from a crosswalk button).

It controls two outputs:
  - light_color, which can be red, yellow, or green
  - walk_signal, a boolean that tells pedestrians whether to walk

The controller has three modes: Green, Yellow, and Red.
It starts in Green mode.

In Green mode, when the timer expires, the light turns yellow and
the walk signal is turned off, then it transitions to Yellow mode.
Otherwise it stays green.

In Yellow mode, when the timer expires, the light turns red and
the walk signal is activated, then it transitions to Red mode.
Otherwise it stays yellow.

In Red mode, when the timer expires, the light turns green and
the walk signal is turned off, then it transitions back to Green mode.
Otherwise it stays red with the walk signal on.

The cycle_time constant is 30 seconds.
